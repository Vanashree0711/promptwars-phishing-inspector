    COMMON QUESTIONS

    Frequently Asked Questions

    🔒 Is my uploaded offer letter stored on any server?

    No. Files are held in RAM (Buffer memory) only for the duration of text extraction and deleted instantly. Nothing is ever written to disk or saved to a database.

    🎯 What makes the Scam Threat Index (0–100%) accurate?

    Unlike black-box AI models that hallucinate, our scoring engine is 100% deterministic and explainable. Every percentage increase is backed by concrete matched evidence text or WHOIS metadata.

    💼 Why do fake offer letters ask for equipment deposits?

    Scammers promise high-paying remote roles, then instruct victims to buy "laptops" or pay "refundable onboarding deposits" via untraceable methods like Zelle, CashApp, or Gift Cards.
